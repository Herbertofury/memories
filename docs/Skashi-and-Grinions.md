## Lore, characters and continuity

The creator's [Grinion Lore](https://skashi95.newgrounds.com/news/post/1530400), published **24 April 2025**, is the primary lore reference. It names **Warai, Little Grin, Smiley Cindy, Sumairu, Bitey and the Brood Mother**.

The article distinguishes the player-dependent Little Laughters timeline from speculative accounts associated with Tritoria. It does not establish one mandatory sequence of fictional events. Warai commands the Grinions; Little Grin and Cindy question the fear-driven way of life, while Sumairu serves Warai. The article is marked as ongoing work, so later creator revisions may matter.

The earlier [Lore of Warai & Grinions post](https://skashi95.newgrounds.com/news/post/1089713), dated **19 March 2020**, links a [creator Q&A video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RUwXwn0TyjM). Its original upload date and current playback were not verified. Do not use the article date as the video's upload date.
