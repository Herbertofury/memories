## Edition relationships
- Super Mario RPG 1996 → Switch remake 2023. [Original English SNES manual](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/clvs/manuals/common/pdf/CLV-P-SABQE.pdf) preserves the full Legend of the Seven Stars title. The March 9 date above is Japanese, not North American.
- Kirby entries are selected anchors from SNES onward, not the complete series and not a claim that every listed game was played. [Official interview](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/News/2017/October/Nintendo-Classic-Mini-SNES-developer-interview-Volume-6-Kirby-Super-Star-1298937.html) distinguishes Super Star/Fun Pak regions.
- Generals base game → Zero Hour expansion. [Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2732960/) dates distribution to 2024-03-07, not original 2003 launch.
- Oblivion 2006 → Remastered 2025. Skyrim 2011 → Special Edition 2016 → Anniversary Edition 2021; do not collapse these into one release date.
- SimCity remains franchise-level until an edition is specified; no optional clarification blocks the rest of the collection.
