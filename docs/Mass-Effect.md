## Order and editions
Original main-game release order: **Mass Effect → Mass Effect 2 → Mass Effect 3 → Mass Effect: Andromeda**.

Legendary Edition is an alternative remastered way to experience the original trilogy, not a fifth story installment. Its [publisher Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1328670/) describes single-player base content and over 40 DLC across 1–3. Andromeda is not included; do not imply all original modes are included.

The first game's later [EA PC listing](https://www.ea.com/games/mass-effect/mass-effect) dates that port to 2008-05-27. This does not replace the 2007 Xbox 360 original date.

Coverage: the requested main games and remastered collection are sourced. Every port, DLC release, official social platform, trailer and video has **not** been enumerated; no exhaustive or playback claim.
