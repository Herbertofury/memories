Source review: 15 November 2026. These entries record user-selected core memories, not ownership of the characters.

## Lucy Lacemaker

**Correct name: Lucy Lacemaker. Creator: Sam Fennah / FENNAH.**

The creator's [SC Lucy Lacemaker (2019) listing](https://fennah.gumroad.com/l/SCLacemaker) identifies an older **Satellite City** model and a newer version used for **The August Few** content. The [creator's bundle](https://fennah.gumroad.com/l/ouitww?layout=profile) corroborates the relationship while distinguishing the older show models from current books and animations. Do not flatten those versions into one unchanged continuity.

[Lucy Lacemaker DANCE!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NTiwU0Gqev4) is an official FENNAH upload dated **3 May 2021**. That is a video's publication date, not Lucy's first appearance. Her exact debut date remains unverified. The creator's [books page](https://fennahbooks.com/books) identifies AMYGDALA and WONDERLUST within The August Few; a character-specific AMYGDALA passage was not retrieved in this pass.

### Rights boundary

The creator states that the character remains theirs and the offered retired models are for personal use. A public product image or official video thumbnail is not an unrestricted image/model license. No model was bought or downloaded, and no user attachment or fan artwork was published here. Permission described for a separate project is not treated as a blanket Memories asset-redistribution grant.

The article distinguishes the fictional application **SCP-1471**, named **MalO**, from the figure **SCP-1471-A** depicted in its images. ## SCP-682

**Source: [SCP-682](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-682), by Dr Gears and Epic Phail Spy, SCP Wiki.**

User-selected favorite character, also known as the Hard-to-Destroy Reptile. The fictional entity is intelligent and reptile-like, with exceptional regeneration and adaptation. Its original publication date remains unverified here; the displayed page revision date is not a debut date.

This adapted SCP-682 description is licensed under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), attributed to both authors and the linked SCP Wiki article.

Its audio is credited to **thattallfellow** under CC BY-SA 3.0. No image, photograph, recording or fan model was imported. These credits do not authorize unrelated SCP-682 artwork or assets.
