## Collection rules
1. Prefer creator-owned channels, official episode pages and playlists.
2. Distinguish **original**, **reupload**, **compilation**, **historical link** and **unverified lead**.
3. Keep original release dates separate from upload/reupload dates. Unknown dates stay unknown.
4. Metadata verification is not playback verification. Broken fetches do not prove deletion.
5. Preserve attribution, not unauthorized copies. Videos, art, characters and trademarks remain their owners' property.
6. No private chat, account data or paywalled content is published.

Reviewed **2026-11-15**. This is an expanding catalog, not a complete episode archive. The supplied creator list is being reconciled; claimed recent activity is not accepted without primary evidence.

Many linked works contain adult language, dark humor, violence or offensive historical titles.
